Latest Work

Projects I have had the pleasure of working on include...

Anonymous Client (NDA)

At TRW I handled a new build in Umbraco 7 for clients and staff of the world's second largest professional services network that featured heavy back office customisation, forum, custom search API integration, and a public/private content split. Dialogue was chosen as the base of the forum and much of the source was modified to allow for additional features such as opening/closing topics, AJAX file uploads, topic taxonomies, and marking posts as solutions in a Stack Overflow style.

I created a multi stage content approval workflow and reporting tool using Angular in the back office enabling different user types to approve & reject content at three stages before being published live. The site was integrated with Site Minder and displayed public/private versions of each page depending on authorisation & authentication status.

299 Lighting

At Wattle I developed a clean Umbraco 7 build for Bristol based lighting retailer 299 utilising the grid datatype which allowed for back office visualisation and control of Bootstrap integration. It included a dynamic product search using AJAX and mustache.js enabling searching within a filtered selection and further filtering of returned results.
